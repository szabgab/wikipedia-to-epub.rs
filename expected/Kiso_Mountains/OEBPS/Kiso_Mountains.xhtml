Kiso Mountains (木曽山脈, Kiso Sanmyaku) are a mountain range↗ in Nagano↗ and Gifu↗ prefectures in Japan↗. They are also called the Central Alps (中央アルプス, Chūō Arupusu) and they combine with the Hida Mountains↗ ("Northern Alps") and the Akaishi Mountains↗ ("Southern Alps") to form a group collectively known as the Japanese Alps↗.

Outline

The mountain range consists of granite↗. The Komagatake Ropeway↗ is on the east side of Mount Kisokoma↗. A lot of tourists visit the station on the top. The upper part of the mountain range is the tree line↗, and a lot of alpine plant↗s grow naturally. Leontopodium shinanense of Leontopodium↗ is endemic↗ around Mount Kisokoma.
